# Review of the deep-research report

The research report is the **reference for product decisions**. This document lists where it is weak,
risky or wrong for building the product, and what the design does about each point. Where the report and
the owner's summary differ, the report wins; I found no material conflict between them.

> File naming: the report is `docs/research-report.md` and the prompt is `docs/research-prompt.md`
> (the names were swapped at first and corrected when the files moved into `docs/`).

| # | Issue in the report | Why it matters | What we do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No sources section** (the prompt required one). Many figures come from snippets: commissions, inventory sizes (Kajaro), HomeToGo numbers. | Unsourced numbers cannot appear in a product whose promise is "no fabricated numbers". | Report figures are context only. Fee policies are sourced from platform pages/checkouts with snapshot ids (ADR-0007 §3). |
| 2 | All-in price assumes we can get "the price for a date and group directly from the platform". | Checkout-level fees often appear only in a booking flow that may need login, which we will not bypass. A per-(date, guests) quote also multiplies crawl volume (≈ 6 scenario requests per listing). | Compute all-in from public nightly + extra-guest rates + sourced fee policy, use direct quotes where public, and otherwise show **ranges/open bounds**. Scenario capture is budgeted in M2/M4 (≈ 6 h per platform at 1 req/3 s). |
| 3 | "Hidden empty night" is framed as a pure win. | A night shown free on one platform and booked on another may be a **stale sync** (host forgot to block), which is an overbooking risk for the user. Measuring H3 is also confounded if the two platforms are observed hours apart. | The UI shows observations with age, not "available". H3 counts only observation pairs < 6 h apart. Calendars on both platforms are captured in the same window. |
| 4 | The storyboard shows exact computed distances ("محاسبه ۱.۸ کیلومتر") although the report itself says exact coordinates are hidden. | A precise distance from an obfuscated point is a fabricated precision. "۵ دقیقه" is also ambiguous (walk or drive). | Distances are **ranges** derived from location precision. `CONTRADICTED` only if even the best case contradicts the claim. The claim's travel mode is extracted; when it is unknown, both modes are evaluated. |
| 5 | Photo-first ER ignores **villa complexes that reuse marketing photos across units**. | These are the hardest negatives: identical photos, different rentable units. A photo-first matcher will merge them, which is the costliest error. | Photo document frequency weighting; shared-photo groups require non-photo evidence; the outcome "same complex, unit unknown" is a non-merge (ADR-0009 §7). |
| 6 | H1 overlap is presented as a direct measurement. | Measured overlap is a **lower bound**: it is limited by ER recall and by crawl coverage on each platform. | Region-complete crawls; coverage reported per platform; H1 is reported with CI and the lower-bound caveat. |
| 7 | "Precision ≥ 95–98%" without a measurement plan beyond "300–500 labelled pairs". | With ~150 predicted positives the 95% CI is roughly ±3.5%. Labels are themselves ambiguous (the "کاخ آرشام" case). | Human gold set with an `unsure` label and a written protocol; Wilson CIs; the operating point is chosen on the lower bound (ADR-0009). |
| 8 | "Drive time in holiday traffic" listed as a feature (#6). | OSRM on OSM gives free-flow times only. There is no traffic data source, so a holiday-traffic number would be invented. Holiday one-way closures (e.g. Chalus road) are not in the routing graph. | Show free-flow time, labelled as such. Any closure note is shown only with a source. |
| 9 | Hybrid retrieval (BM25 + dense + RRF) and OpenSearch proposed for 1.5–3k documents. | Overkill: hard filters (capacity, dates, geo) reduce candidates to tens or hundreds. | Postgres FTS first; dense retrieval only if it wins the M8 eval by a set margin (ADR-0006). |
| 10 | Jabama (and Kajaro) blocked automated access during research; robots.txt was never read for any platform. | The four-platform scope may shrink. The OCP proof in M2 depends on a second permitted platform. | Robots/ToS audit is the first M2 task, with owner sign-off. Plan B uses otaghak or shab. |
| 11 | Commission "mental model" (guest pays 1.20N / 1.24N / 1.33N). | It is an inference, not data. It is fine for strategy and wrong for the product. | Not used in pricing; the M3 H2 report measures the real differences. |
| 12 | Hazm/Parsivar suggested for Persian processing. | Heavy dependencies for what is mostly character, digit and ZWNJ normalization plus a gazetteer. | In-house normalizer in the shared kernel (pure, property-tested). |
| 13 | Cross-platform republishing (Alibaba/Snapptrip showing jabama inventory) noted as an open question. | Republished listings are not independent evidence of multi-platform presence and would inflate H1. | Out of the 4-platform scope. If one is added later, it gets a `republishes: <platform>` source profile flag and is excluded from overlap statistics. |
| 14 | Storyboard numbers ("۲۱۳ نظر", "۹.۲ میلیون", "۷ از ۸ عکس") read like results. | They are illustrative, not data. | The demo script will only use numbers from generated reports (ROADMAP M11 criterion 2). |
