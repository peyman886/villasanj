## Overview

<CriteriaProgress locale="en" />

Statuses:

<span className="inline-flex flex-wrap gap-2 align-middle">
  <StatusBadge status="done" locale="en" />
  <StatusBadge status="waived" locale="en" />
  <StatusBadge status="partial" locale="en" />
  <StatusBadge status="provisional" locale="en" />
  <StatusBadge status="owner_review" locale="en" />
  <StatusBadge status="deferred" locale="en" />
  <StatusBadge status="blocked" locale="en" />
  <StatusBadge status="not_met" locale="en" />
</span>

"Closed by the owner" means the criterion did not reach its written target or could not be done,
and the owner explicitly closed it; it is not "done" and is counted separately. "Partly done" means
at least one criterion is open. "Provisional" means measured, but on data that has not been
reviewed yet. "Blocked" means an external obstacle (permission, a missing source). "Not met" means
measured and short of the target; such a criterion is never hidden.
