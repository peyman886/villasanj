## Reviews

- Reviews are parsed from the same listing-page snapshots; reviewer names are not stored.
- The guest rating is adjusted with a **Bayesian prior**: the platform average as the default, so
  that two five-star votes do not weigh as much as a hundred. Ranking uses this adjusted rating.
- A Jabama rating of "0" means "no rating", not a rating of zero.
- On the villa page every review carries its platform label and stay date and gets a direct link,
  so the summary's citations are clickable.

    A point without a citation, a citation to a review that was not in the input, or a raw digit in

    never shows anything uncited.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Display">
    The summary arrives under Suspense after the rest of the page; every point links to the reviews
    it cites.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Quality and cost

- **The owner's blind review (M10 criterion 3):** 20 summaries next to all the raw reviews, without
  seeing which model or prompt wrote them: **20 of 20 faithful** (criterion ≥ 18).
- **Passing the verifier:** 48 of 48 summaries on the first attempt (0 retries, 0 dropped points).
- **Reasoning setting:** gemini-3.8-flash "thinks" by default. On the same two listings, `low` cut
  the cost from $0.0153 to $0.0035 and the latency from about 6 to 2.7 s, with the same points and
  citations. The later blind review confirmed this setting.
- **Estimate before running:** after calibrating on 24 listings, the dry-run estimate on 24 other
  listings was within 3% of the cost ledger (M9 criterion 5: ±25%).

## Reproduce

```bash
cd backend
uv run villasanj catalog reviews                                  # review coverage
uv run villasanj enrichment summarize jabama <id> --dry-run       # cost estimate, no calls
uv run villasanj enrichment summary-review-eval                   # blind review result
```
