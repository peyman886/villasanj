  where the hardest part is that a villa has no shared identifier across platforms.

## The problem

Someone looking for a weekend villa in the north of Iran sees the same villa on several platforms
with different prices, calendars and descriptions. The advertised price is not the final price:
extra guests, holiday nights and fees are added later. The calendars are not in sync: a night that
is booked on one platform shows as free on another. And nobody has checked claims such as "5
minutes to the sea".

The product is built around three questions:

1. **Are these listings the same villa?** If we merge the listings of two different villas, we
   send the user to the wrong villa. So precision comes before recall: the bar is precision ≥ 95%
   with a lower bound ≥ 92%.
2. **For my dates and group size, what does each platform really charge?** Every part of the price
   with its source; when a part is unknown we show a range or a "from" price, never an invented
   number.
3. **Which of the listing's claims match the evidence?** In the tone of "not confirmed", never an
   accusation.

## Non-negotiable rules

These six rules are enforced by the code and the tests, not just by this document:

1. **No invented number.** Every price or claim has provenance (platform, snapshot, observation
   time). Unknown means a range or a "from" price; we never make up a cap. <Adr n="0007" locale="en" />
2. **The LLM writes no digits.** Facts go in as IDs, the model writes `{slot}`s in their place, and
   a deterministic renderer and verifier fill and check them.
3. **Prices are never merged.** Each listing in a villa keeps its own offer.
4. **A villa has at most one listing per platform**, enforced both by the domain aggregate and by
   a unique index in the database.
5. **The truth check says "not confirmed".** "Contradicted by the evidence" is used only when even
   the most favourable reading of the evidence rules the claim out.
6. **Availability and prices are observations, not states.** Each carries its observation time and
   age.

## Scope and limits

- **Region:** Ramsar to Tonekabon (plus the surrounding villages in the gazetteer).
- **Platforms:** Jabama and Shab. Jajiga, Otaghak and Mihmansho forbid crawling in their terms of
  service and are excluded until they give written permission. <Adr n="0011" locale="en" />
- **Hardware:** one Apple M4 laptop with 16 GB of memory; everything runs locally, with no GPU in
  Docker. <Adr n="0003" locale="en" />
- **LLM cost:** a hard cap of $30, with a cost ledger and an estimate before every run.
  <Adr n="0004" locale="en" />
- **Fees:** neither platform publishes its guest fee, so every offer is a "from" price.
