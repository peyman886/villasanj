## Closed by the owner

These criteria did not reach their written target or could not be done, and the owner closed them.
Their status in the milestone table is "Closed by the owner", not "Done".

- **New platforms (M4 criterion 1, 5 Oct):** the owner wants no new platform. Jajiga, Otaghak and
  Mihmansho forbid crawling in their terms of service; the scope stays Jabama and Shab.
  <Adr n="0011" locale="en" />
- **Comparison with a direct quote (M6 criterion 3, 5 Oct):** neither platform publishes the final
  amount on a public page; offers stay lower bounds ("from").
- **Explanation latency without the cache (M10 criterion 4, 5 Oct):** the uncached p95 is 6.1 to
  10.6 seconds against a target of 4 seconds; the owner accepted the latency with the cache.
  <Adr n="0005" locale="en" />
- **Relevance evaluation (M8 criterion 2, 8 Oct):** 11 queries were fully judged instead of 30, and
  the owner closed the criterion with these; the current ranking beats both baselines
  ([search](/en/docs/search-ranking)).

The owner reconciled the cost ledger with the AvalAI dashboard (M1 criterion 8 and M11 criterion 3);
those two are "Done". A UX review of the villa page (M7) was not an acceptance criterion and the
owner did not do it. The offline demo shows photos from the platforms' servers (A24) and has no
local copy.

## Language model work

With the new AvalAI credit (5 to 8 Oct, about $0.4 in total):

- The only unjudged pair in the judge's zone was judged ("not the same"; the villas did not change).
- The relevance judging queue was built (30 query understanding calls) and the cache for the demo
  paths was warmed.
- Query understanding was measured on the owner's reviewed set without the cache: 99.3% slots, zero
  invented numbers, p95 about 2.1 seconds (M8 criterion 1 is done).
- A vision-model check for sea view and fireplace was built and measured on 336 labelled photos; it
  did not reach 85% and is not used ([truth check](/en/docs/truth-check)). We did not run it on the
  75 listings that make the claim, because its result would not be used anywhere.

## Known limits of the methods

| Limit                                                    | Effect                                                                                   | What compensates                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The judge was chosen on the same gold set pairs          | End-to-end precision of judge-backed policies is optimistic                              | Judge merges are off; the gold-v2 sample is the clean test                                                   |
| The gold set's geographic stratum has about 30 pairs     | The lower bound on the precision of judge merges below the threshold is wide             | The er-human queue: 70 of 72 judge suggestions confirmed (lower bound about 90%); judge auto-merge stays off |
| Only five cover photos per listing                       | Pairs whose photos show different rooms stay "unsure"                                    | The human queue; more photos only where they would settle a pair                                             |
| Free-flow drive times                                    | Optimistic on a busy weekend                                                             | A "no traffic" label next to every time (A10)                                                                |
| An assumed 500 m blur circle for Shab                    | Some claims' verdicts rest on an assumption                                              | The `radius_assumed` flag on every row (A14)                                                                 |
| An incomplete map of shops and restaurants               | These destinations can only be confirmed, never contradicted                             | A rule in code, not in prose                                                                                 |
| Every offer is a lower bound ("from")                    | The real total with fees is unknown                                                      | No invented cap; the H2 comparison is without fees                                                           |
| The query set was written by the agent                   | The owner reviewed and approved it, but the queries are not from real users              | Measure query understanding again on real user queries                                                       |
| Lunar holidays come only from Jabama's flag              | Beyond the observed horizon they are unknown                                             | An "unknown lunar holidays" caveat instead of a guess                                                        |
| Search with cached understanding takes about two seconds | It slows down with every calendar capture (the holiday-flag query scans the whole table) | A profile in [search](/en/docs/search-ranking); no target was set                                            |
