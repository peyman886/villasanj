## Data and clip

- **Source:** the Geofabrik Iran extract, snapshot `iran-260930` (230 MB, MD5 checked). The
  snapshot name is stored on every derived row in the `dataset` column.
- **Clip:** `osmium extract` with the box from longitude 49.4 to 52.6 and latitude 35.4 to 37.6:
  Tehran, the Chalus, Haraz and Qazvin to Rasht roads, and the coast from Rasht to Amol. Every
  Jabama listing and 597 of 601 Shab listings fall inside the box.
- **Tooling:** osmium runs in a small Debian image, so nothing is installed on the host. The data
  licence is ODbL, with attribution at the foot of the pages.

## Blurred location and intervals

Jabama publishes a 400 m blur radius and Shab gives no radius at all. For the distance to a fixed
shape, the interval is exact: every point within radius r of the pin lies between d − r and d + r
from that shape. When a platform gives no radius, **we assume 500 m** and the row carries a
`radius_assumed` flag so the interface can say which verdicts rest on that assumption (A14).
Entity resolution reads the pin as exact, because there it only affects recall.

## Coastline

The 55 `natural=coastline` ways inside the box are stored as `geography(LineString)` with a GiST
index in `enrichment.coastline`, and distances are geodesic (`ST_Distance` on geography). Median
distance to the coast: 2.2 km for Jabama and 1.6 km for Shab (3 October 2026). Listings whose title
says "beachfront" are 1 to 5 m from the line; "forest" ones are up to 26 km away.

## Places

From the same snapshot we extract shops, bakeries, restaurants, medical centres, shopping centres,
town and village centres and forests (about 18 thousand places in `enrichment.place`), and for
every listing we store the nearest place of each kind with its blur interval. Two rules came out of
a manual review of the verdicts:

1. **An incomplete map can only confirm.** A shop missing from OSM means the map lacks it, not that
   the shop does not exist. For shops, restaurants, medical centres and forests, the nearest place
   on the map is an upper bound on the real distance: it confirms the claim or gives "Not
   confirmed", and never contradicts it.
2. **Town centres are complete and can contradict.** Every gazetteer town has a centre in OSM. A
   centre is an area, not a point: anywhere within 1.5 km of the town's point counts as "the
   centre" (A20). In the 3 October 2026 run, without this rule, 210 town-centre claims were
   contradicted; the manual review of those same claims set the size of this area. The current
   count is in the table below.

## Drive time

- **OSRM** version 6 on an MLD graph of the same clip, under the `routing` compose profile (about
  287 MB of memory). The "from Tehran" origin is Azadi Square (A16).
- For each listing, the pin and 8 points on the blur circle are routed; the minimum, the pin time
  and the maximum are stored in `discovery.drive_time`. A villa's time is the union of its
  listings' intervals.
- **No traffic:** we have no traffic data, so no "holiday traffic" factor is invented and every
  time is labelled "without traffic" (A10).
- Coverage: 100% of listings with coordinates (M8 criterion 3). Median from Azadi Square: 261
  minutes; the mean width of the blur interval is 3.3 minutes and its 95th percentile 6.5 minutes.

## Offline map

The listing page's map is MapLibre. `make basemap` builds a Protomaps extract of the region (about
11 MB, zoom 0 to 15) with glyphs and sprites in `data/basemap`, served from `/basemap/*` with byte
ranges; labels are in Persian and no external tile is loaded. If the basemap has not been built,
the map falls back to OSM tiles with attribution. The pin and its blur circle are drawn on the map;
an assumed circle is dashed.

<Callout kind="caution" title="Limitations">
  Times without traffic are optimistic on busy weekends, and they are labelled as such. The 500 m
  assumption for Shab is a judgement call and is visible on every row. Targets without a kind
  (recreation, shrines, named places) stay "Not checked".
</Callout>

## Reproduce

```bash
make osm-download osm-prepare   # Geofabrik snapshot, clip, coastline, places and OSRM graph
make routing-up                 # OSRM (the core stack is untouched)
make geo                        # load into PostGIS, distances, drive times, verdicts
make basemap                    # offline basemap
```
