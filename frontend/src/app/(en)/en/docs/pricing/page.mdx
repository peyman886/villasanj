## The formula

```text
total = Σ nightly price of each night
      + max(0, guests − base capacity) × extra-guest rate × nights
      + fees per the platform's fee policy (unknown ⇒ open upper bound)
```

- Every component is a `MoneyRange` with a source. A component with no source yields an **open
  upper bound**: "from X". We never invent a cap.
- A night's price comes from the calendar observation for that night. If the calendar has no price
  for it, the listing's rate card (regular, weekend, holiday) is used and the offer says so as a
  caveat; if the platform left a rate "not set", it is unknown, not zero.
- Each offer's kind is explicit: `EXACT` (every component known), `RANGE` (a closed range) or
  `OPEN` ("from X").
- The engine gives no offer in these cases and says why: too small for the group, below the
  minimum nights, a night seen booked or closed, a night with no usable observation.
- Branch coverage of `pricing/domain` is enforced at 100% in `make test`, and a property test
  makes sure every component of every offer has a source.

## Fees: why every offer is a "from" price

Neither platform publishes the guest fee or the final total on its public pages, and the checkout
page is not crawled (ADR-0008). So each platform's fee policy is `fees_known = false`, with a source
that says what was checked:

<ConfigSnippet file="fees.toml" locale="en" />

The result: every bookable offer is "from X", the most precise statement the data allows. A
comparison with the platform's direct price (M6 criterion 3) was not possible, because no public
source of the final price exists; the owner confirmed this and closed the criterion (5 October
2026).

## Per-person share

Every offer also carries the per-person share (`per_person`). The division rounds the range
outwards so the real share always stays inside it, and an open total gives an open share. Search
compares the budget against this share, per person and per night.

## Prices are never merged

A villa listed on both platforms has two offers, each with its own source, side by side. There is

## Freshness

Prices and availability are observations, not states. Every offer carries the observation time of
its oldest input and, when that is more than 24 hours old, gets a `stale` flag in the API; the page
states the age of every observation ("seen free on Jabama 3 hours ago").

## The merged calendar

The villa page shows each platform's calendar side by side. A night that is free on one platform
and booked on the other is a "hidden night": either one calendar is out of sync, or the villa was
booked through another channel. Villasanj does not say which one is right; it shows both with
their age and treats this as a warning sign, not a guarantee.
