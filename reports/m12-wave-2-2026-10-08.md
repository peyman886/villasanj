## Decisions made in this wave

- A supported feature claim is labelled «در عکس‌ها دیده شد», except near-sea claims, which the map
  supports («روی نقشه تأیید شد»). At most two claim highlights, so the measured distance, drive time
  and ratings also fit in five.
- «consistent» (description agrees with the amenity list) sits in «تأیید نشد»: both are the
  host's word (A18). Its badge still says «با فهرست امکانات می‌خواند».
- The summary review tool (`/label/summaries`, closed queue) keeps single-review points visible so
  the owner could judge faithfulness; product pages hide them.
- The header's «مستندات پروژه» became a quiet «برای داوران» link; the docs portal is unchanged.
- Arabic letters join across inline elements: a badge right after a word changed its final letter
  («توماڈ»). A space now separates text from inline badges and chips (copy polish, also wave 3).
