## Demo villa (decisions.md D8.4)

Rule applied by `scripts/pick_demo_villa.py` over all 377 two-platform villas, window 2026-10-08 +
30 nights. The strict rule first picked `v-20175a9b8bbb`, whose shab listing was unavailable for the
whole month (30 of 30 nights "hidden"; no second price for the demo stay). Two conditions were added
(A26): both platforms bookable for the demo search's stay, and hidden nights ≤ half the window.
Result: **`v-6331f454983f`** (ویلا دوخوابه اطلس سفید تمشک): 4 hidden nights in 30, area stated
as 356 and 130 m, 3 shared photo pairs, 12 written reviews with a cited summary, both platforms
bookable (jabama از ۸٬۵۰۰٬۰۰۰، shab از ۹٬۰۰۰٬۰۰۰ for 2 nights, 6 people). No relaxation needed.

## Decisions made in this wave (no owner questions, per M12)

- «دست‌کم» in the LLM explanation's slot text instead of «از» (a running sentence; A25).
- Demo villa rule extended (A26). Calendar = two Jalali months; default stay = first scenario (A27).
- Budget flip as a `basis:` edit, no new LLM call (A28). Rounding steps for durations and distances (A29).
- Dates and guests are edited on the villa page (booking card); the search chips stay removable,
  not editable, because changing them would need a second query path (recorded for wave 3).
- Single-platform search cards link to «جزئیات ویلا» (there is nothing to compare).

## Tech debt and notes

- Pins are not clustered; at the region's zoom some overlap (wave 3 adds "search this area").
- The calendar fetches 60 days but shows two months; nights outside them still count in the API.
- shab marks nights beyond its booking horizon as unavailable, so late-window "hidden nights" can
  be a horizon artefact; the page shows what was observed and does not interpret it.
